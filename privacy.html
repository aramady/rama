Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Rama Studio is a private desktop application used only by its owner. It has no servers and no public users.

What it uses

When the owner connects an account (for example TikTok), the app receives the account's basic profile (display name, avatar, open ID) and access tokens with the permissions the owner granted, such as uploading videos as drafts.

Where it is kept

Tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, encrypted with the operating system's secure storage. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to anyone other than the platform itself (TikTok, Facebook or YouTube) to carry out the owner's own uploads.

Removing access

Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its tokens. Access can also be revoked at any time in the platform's account settings (on TikTok: Settings › Security › Manage app permissions).

YouTube API Services

Rama Studio uses YouTube API Services to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels and to set their title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions, playlist and visibility. By using the YouTube features of Rama Studio you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

YouTube data the app accesses: the connected channel's ID, title and thumbnail; the list of the channel's own uploaded videos (ID, title, upload status, privacy status) so the app can find a video the owner uploaded and apply its settings; playlists of the channel; and the daily API quota used. With the owner's permission the app uploads videos, updates video metadata, sets thumbnails and captions, and adds videos to playlists.

How it is used, stored and shared: this data is used only inside the app, only to publish the owner's own content. OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, encrypted with the operating system's secure storage; channel and video IDs are kept locally next to the owner's projects. No YouTube data is sent to any server of ours (there is none), sold, or shared with any third party. The app does not use cookies or any tracking on the owner's device.

Deleting data and revoking access: disconnecting a channel in the app deletes its tokens and stored YouTube data immediately. Access can also be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page; the app then deletes the stored channel data the next time it notices the revocation (and at the latest within 7 days). Locally stored YouTube data is refreshed or deleted within 30 days at most.
